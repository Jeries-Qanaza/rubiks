The 2 Essential "Triggers" Every Speedcuber Must Memorize

These short 4-move sequences form 80% of all speedcubing algorithms. Practice them until they are instant!

1. Sexy Move R U R' U'

Right index flicks U, Right thumb pulls down R', Left index flicks U'. Executed in under 0.4 seconds by pros.

2. Sledgehammer R' F R F'

Pull down R' with right thumb, push Front face F with right index, reset R, push F' with left index.
